# markerdata 0.1.0

* Initial release. Bundles `SNP55K_maize282_maf04` and serves seven curated
  12,000-SNP panels (cattle, eucalyptus, poplar, rice, sorghum, soybean,
  tetraploid wheat) on demand through `marker_data()`, with a local cache,
  a catalog (`marker_catalog()`) and citation objects (`marker_references()`).
